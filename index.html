What to wear

Coastal formal,
and sturdy shoes.

Suits and long dresses, dressed for weather rather than a ballroom. Bring a layer you can put on and take off. Expect wind off the Atlantic and mornings to start cool.

Footwear, seriously

Flats, boots or block heels with good grip. We'll be standing on uneven surfaces, with a short walk in.

Travel & lodging

Getting there, and where to sleep

By air
Bangor is closest

BGR is about 55 minutes from Bar Harbor. Portland is a pretty three hours up the coast, and if fares are better, Boston and Manchester are both a five-hour drive.

Where to stay
Book sooner than feels necessary

We recommend staying in Bar Harbor. Early June starts the season and the accommodations fill fast. We'd hold something refundable now and refine later. Any room blocks we arrange will be posted here.

On the island
Getting around

Bar Harbor has a walkable downtown with many shops and dining options to explore. The free island shuttle starts in June, but with limited routes, so some park destinations will require a car.

Recommended stays
Options in town
Bar Harbor Inn The Bar Harbor Villager Motel Bar Harbor Grand

All three are in town, with access to Island Explorer Shuttle stops and restaurants. There are Airbnb options all around the island. The other towns on the island are lovely, but may require more driving to get to some of the weekend's events.

For those planning a longer stay, Bar Harbor/Acadia Cottage Rentals has well priced options loved by locals.

Three places we'd send you first

Bringing the family

Things to do with kids

There are lots of kid friendly options on Mount Desert Island. Here a few to get you started.

Half a day
Tide pools at Ship Harbor

A flat figure-eight loop out to the rocks. Go at low tide and count crabs, sea stars and periwinkles. Rubber boots are a good idea.

An hour or three
Carriage roads by bike

No cars, gentle grades, stone bridges to stop at. Shops in Bar Harbor rent trailers, tag-alongs and small frames — the Eagle Lake loop is the easy classic.

Rainy morning
Ranger programs

Sieur de Monts has a small nature center, wild gardens and a Junior Ranger booklet kids can finish in an afternoon to earn their Junior Ranger badge.

Late afternoon
Echo Lake, then ice cream

Warmer than the ocean, with a sandy beach and a lifeguard in season. Dry clothes in the car, then a cone on Main Street in Bar Harbor.
